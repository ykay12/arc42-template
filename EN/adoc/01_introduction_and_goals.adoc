== Introduction and Goals

=== Requirements Overview
 
The Image Sharing App is a mobile-first platform targeting photography enthusiasts and professionals on iOS and Android. It enables users to share, discover, and edit high-quality images within a community-driven environment that differentiates itself from general social media through advanced editing tools, customisable filters, and structured community features such as challenges and competitions.
 
Key functional requirements derived from the Business Requirements Document:
 
* Users can register, log in, and manage personal profiles and image collections.
* Users can upload images, add titles and descriptions, and tag them for search and discovery.
* Users can apply advanced editing tools (colour correction, white balance, exposure, cropping, text overlays, borders) and customisable filters — including filters created and saved by the user.
* Images can be shared to external platforms (Facebook, Instagram).
* Users can follow other users, comment on and like images, and participate in community challenges and competitions.
* The app integrates with Pixlr (cloud-based photo editing SaaS) for additional editing capability without leaving the platform.
* A freemium subscription model provides a free tier with limited features and monthly/annual paid tiers with full access.
